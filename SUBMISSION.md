Title: Photo Challenge

Notes / Description:
Built a reusable two-entry photo challenge with honest evidence boundaries. The host freezes textual criteria, designated left and right entrants submit descriptions plus provenance declarations, and GenLayer validators return two criterion masks and a bounded comparison. Each entrant can object once, while a separate human judge alone records the winner. The contract never claims to inspect pixels, metadata, authorship, time, or place.
